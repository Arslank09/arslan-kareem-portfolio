01 About

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at Federal Urdu University, Islamabad, and a Junior AI Engineer at EHB Technologies, shipping LLM assistants and search features to live, production web services.

My work is mostly in applied AI: tool-calling LLM agents built with LangChain and LangGraph, multi-provider model support, retrieval-augmented generation, semantic search, and multilingual (English / Roman Urdu) conversation. Alongside that I build the systems underneath them: NestJS microservices, PostgreSQL, Redis, WebSockets and webhook-driven integrations. I care about making AI features safe, measurable and dependable in production.
